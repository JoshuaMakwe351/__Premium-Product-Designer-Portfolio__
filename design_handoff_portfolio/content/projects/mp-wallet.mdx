overview: MP Wallet simplifies sending, receiving, converting and withdrawing USDC through a secure mobile experience — making stablecoin money movement feel as familiar as a banking app.
problem: "Crypto UX intimidates first-time and non-technical users. Jargon, irreversible mistakes and opaque fees erode the trust required to move real money. MP Wallet needed to lower that barrier without hiding what's actually happening on-chain."
goals:
  - Build trust
  - Simplify crypto
  - Reduce friction
  - Improve onboarding
researchIntro: I studied both crypto-native and mainstream finance apps to borrow the trust cues of familiar banking while avoiding the intimidation of exchange interfaces.
research: [Coinbase, Binance, Cash App, Revolut, Trust Wallet]
process: "I storyboarded the highest-anxiety moments — receiving to the right address, ramping in and out of fiat, confirming a send — and prototyped each until the flow felt reassuring. Dual-currency framing (USD and NGN) was validated early as the fastest way to build confidence."
features: [Authentication, Dashboard, Send, Receive, On-ramp, Off-ramp, KYC, Transaction History, Biometric Auth]
decisions:
  - h: "Dual-currency everywhere."
    p: "Showing balances and conversions in both USD and local currency removed the mental math that stops non-technical users cold."
  - h: "Loud warnings, quiet chrome."
    p: "Address-mismatch and irreversible-action warnings are impossible to miss; everything else stays calm."
  - h: "Banking patterns over crypto patterns."
    p: "On/off-ramp flows mirror deposits and withdrawals people already understand."
