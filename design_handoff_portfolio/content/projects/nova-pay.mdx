challenge: >
  Designing a banking experience that feels powerful enough for experienced
  crypto users while remaining approachable for someone completely new to
  digital finance.

solution: >
  I created a clean visual hierarchy, familiar banking patterns, simplified
  user flows and premium visual styling that reduces cognitive load while
  maintaining functionality.

  Nova Pay delivers a modern fintech experience combining traditional banking
  with digital assets in one intuitive application. The interface prioritises
  speed, trust and simplicity while maintaining a premium visual identity.

  Great fintech design is built on clarity rather than complexity. Reducing
  friction and using familiar interaction patterns makes sophisticated
  financial products accessible to more users.
